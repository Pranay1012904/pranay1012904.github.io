## 📖 Description
Auto-Stress-Calm is a Wear OS app designed to help you identify and reduce stress with minimal battery drain. Using data from your Fitbit sensors, it automatically detects when you're stressed. The app then gently guides your breathing through haptic feedback. This simple process helps you regain calmness and maintain mindfulness throughout your day.

## 🚀 Getting Started
Follow these easy steps to download and set up Auto-Stress-Calm on your Wear OS smartwatch.

### 📦 System Requirements
- **Compatible Devices:** Any smartwatch running Wear OS
- **Battery Life:** Minimal impact on battery during use
- **Fitbit Device:** A connected Fitbit for stress detection

### 🔗 Download & Install
1. Visit this page to download: [Auto-Stress-Calm Releases](https://github.com/Pranay1012904/Auto-Stress-Calm/releases).
2. Look for the most recent version. Click on the version number to view the release details.
3. Download the latest APK file for your smartwatch.
4. Transfer the APK to your smartwatch if necessary. You can use a file manager app on your phone to do this.
5. On your smartwatch, find the downloaded APK file and tap on it to begin the installation.
6. If prompted, allow installations from unknown sources to ensure the app installs correctly.

### ⚙️ Setup
1. Open Auto-Stress-Calm on your smartwatch after installation.
2. Connect your smartwatch with your Fitbit device to enable stress detection.
3. Follow the on-screen prompts to set up your preferences.

### 🌟 Features
- **Automatic Stress Detection:** Uses Fitbit sensors to recognize stress levels.
- **Guided Breathing:** Provides haptic feedback to guide your breathing exercises.
- **Battery Friendly:** Designed to work with near-zero battery impact.
- **Privacy-Focused:** Your data remains on your devices and is not shared with third parties.

### 🛠 Troubleshooting
If you experience any issues while using the app, refer to these common resolutions:

- **Installation Problems:** Ensure you have allowed installations from unknown sources in your smartwatch settings.
- **Connection Issues:** Check that your Fitbit device is properly paired with your smartwatch.
- **Haptic Feedback Not Working:** Make sure your smartwatch settings allow for haptic alerts.

For more advanced concerns, feel free to raise an issue in the GitHub repository.
